Ordering FAQs

Can I inquire without a complete design?

Yes. Share your intended use, preferred style and approximate quantity. We can discuss the next steps.

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

MOQ depends on the fabric, style, customization and production conditions. Please inquire for project-specific evaluation.

Can BOYU assist with sampling?

Sampling and product development can be discussed based on project scope, materials and technical requirements.

How are prices and timelines determined?

They depend on specifications, quantity, materials and production scheduling. We provide estimates after reviewing your requirements.
